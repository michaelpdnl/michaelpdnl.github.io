We aim to explain what drives private housing sale prices and rents in Hong Kong, and how these diverge across the 18 districts and over the past decade. Using official statistics combined with listing-level data collected from real estate agency websites, we built 3 interactive dashboards that support visual analysis on price formation, spatial disparity, and the relationship between rents, prices and affordability. 

Three topics we wish to explore: 
| Topics | Analyses |
| ------ | -------- |
| Prices  | Influencing factors of housing and rental prices, as well as spatial and temporal disparities  |
| Financing  | Rental yield and investment patterns across districts and property types  |
| Affordability  | Comparing income against price and rent, identifying strategies for tenants and buyers  |

This is a collective effort that stems from a group assignment in the Visualization and visual analytics course. I am the proposer of this idea.
