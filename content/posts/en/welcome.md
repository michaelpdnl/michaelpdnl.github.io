This is the first post on my new site. I plan to use the blog for short daily
thoughts: small lessons, interesting problems, and things I am learning.

A few ground rules I want to keep for myself:

- Write in both English and 中文.
- Keep posts short — a daily thought, not an essay.
- Ship often; a post pushed is better than a perfect draft.

The site is fully static: every post here is a Markdown file in the repository,
and publishing is just a `git push`. You can read the technical details in the
[docs](https://github.com/michaelpdnl/michaelpdnl.github.io/tree/main/docs).
